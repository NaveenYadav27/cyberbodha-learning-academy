## 1. Principles of Truthful Positioning

To maintain enterprise credibility and academic respect, CyberBodha enforces strict guidelines against deceptive educational claims:

| Deceptive Practice to Avoid | CyberBodha Enforced Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Guaranteed Job Placements ("100% Placement Guarantee")** | **Capability-Driven Readiness:** We clearly state that we do not promise guaranteed jobs or fixed salaries. We provide structured training, verified lab scenarios, portfolio building, and interview preparation. |
| **Fabricated Student Reviews & Avatars** | **Verified Pedagogical Demonstrations:** No fake reviews, fictional student testimonials, or unverified star ratings appear on the public academy pages. |
| **Unverified Accreditations** | **Accurate Examination Notes:** Programs preparing learners for third-party industry benchmarks (e.g., CEH, CompTIA, Splunk) clearly specify that external certification exams are independently administered. |
| **Fabricated Client / Partner Logos** | **Zero Unauthorized Partner Claims:** We do not display logos of fortune 500 enterprises or universities without formal, executed written partnership agreements. |
| **Confusing Simulation with Production Access** | **Explicit Range Disclaimers:** All virtual lab sections explicitly state that exercises run inside strictly segregated, authorized virtual environments. |

## 2. Content Review by Page

### 1. Homepage (`/`)
- Retains authentic brand headline: *"Learn to defend what matters."*
- Learning pathways clearly explain audience, skills developed, and recommended courses without hyperbolic salary claims.
- Demonstrates the CyberBodha 6-step learning journey: *Explore → Learn → Practice → Assess → Build Skills → Advance*.

### 2. Course Detail Pages (`/courses/:slug`)
- Clear breakdown of modules, topics, practical lab highlights, and prerequisites.
- "Enquire About Course" modal provides honest next steps rather than high-pressure countdown timers or fake discounts.
- Transparent note on delivery format: *"Instructor-led training with hands-on Cyber Range labs."*

### 3. Cyber Range Showcase (`/cyber-range`)
- Clearly distinguishes educational virtual labs from real production networks.
- Outlines ethical frameworks, authorized testing boundaries, and compliance alignment.

### 4. Corporate & University Portals (`/corporate-training` & `/university-programs`)
- Professional institutional consultation forms.
- Realistic descriptions of train-the-trainer workshops, semester elective integration, and team skill diagnostics.
